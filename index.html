Nathanson Lab, Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania

Philadelphia, PA

June 2024 – August 2026
  • Conducted association testing on approximately 500K single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) across thousands of samples in a genome-wide association study (GWAS) meta-analysis of polygenic risk scores and cancer relapse in testicular germ cell tumors, producing Manhattan and QQ plots to assess genome-wide significance

Enhancing Genetic Ancestry Inference through TDA and Clustering

Wu, J., & Yang, T. — Poster, Intelligent Systems for Molecular Biology (ISMB) Conference

July 2026
  • Paired topological features with unsupervised clustering to improve ancestry inference in admixed populations, reaching 96% accuracy on the admixed American (AMR) superpopulation versus 73% for PCA with k-means
  • Applied persistent homology to 1000 Genomes Project SNP data from 2,504 individuals across 26 populations, generating persistence barcodes to capture population structure beyond what linear methods such as PCA reveal
  • Maintained overall ancestry classification accuracy of 99.08% using topological data analysis (TDA) with Ward linkage clustering, on par with PCA with k-means (99%)

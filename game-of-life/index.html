Conway's Game of Life

Draw cells, load classic patterns, and watch generations evolve under Conway's four rules.

Rules

  • Underpopulation: live cell with fewer than 2 neighbors dies.
  • Survival: live cell with 2 or 3 neighbors lives.
  • Overpopulation: live cell with more than 3 neighbors dies.
  • Reproduction: dead cell with exactly 3 neighbors becomes alive.

Click or drag on the grid to toggle cells.
